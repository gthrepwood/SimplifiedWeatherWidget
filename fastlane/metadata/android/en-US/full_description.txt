A simple, lightweight Android weather widget inspired by clean design.

Features:
- Simplified Weather Widget: Clean and straightforward weather display at a glance.
- Minimal Permissions: Requests little permission (only location when needed) for local weather.
- Opaque Design: Solid, distraction-free opaque background.
- Dark & Light Mode: Seamlessly works in both dark and light modes.
- Simple & Small: Compact footprint that takes up minimal space on your screen.
